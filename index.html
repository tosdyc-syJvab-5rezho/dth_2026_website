Introduction to OSS

This page introduces the concept of open-source software, explaining how it works, why it has become widely used, and its advantages.

OSS Disadvantages

OSS presents also disavatngaes as they can involve extra maintainance and trainign costs, be difficult to use, lack of professional support, and risk becoming outdated if developers stop maintaining it.

CLARIN Case

An example of Open Source Software (OSS) is the Virtual Language Observatory (VLO), developed by CLARIN ERIC. It provides free access to digital language resources, such as text collections and speech corpora, helping researchers find and analyse multilingual materials.
